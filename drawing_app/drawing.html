Dave's Sketch Pad instructions

Tools: Draw makes marks; Text adds editable text; Fill floods an area. Wash uses 30% opacity. Click Fill again or press Escape to return to Draw. The eyedropper beside Zoom previews the hovered hex color, samples a canvas color on click, turns Wash off, and exits with Escape.

Select and import: Press S to select; press it twice quickly to switch Rectangle and Free-form. Drag selections to move them. An active selection crops an import.

Shortcuts: Shift constrains; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift or Ctrl/Cmd+Alt make a square/rectangle; add X for a circle/ellipse. Space floats a selection; L outlines a selection; E opens effects; F toggles Fill; M mirrors; W toggles Wash; T toggles theme; R restores defaults. Arrow keys change color (left/right) or size (up/down); 1/2/3/4 do the same outside text entry. Ctrl/Cmd+C/X/V copies, cuts, and pastes; Ctrl+Z/Y or Cmd+Z/Shift+Cmd+Z undoes and redoes.

Preferences: Save color, Wash, selection mode, default theme, line width, and text size.

Help: Float keeps Help above the canvas. Drag its title bar, double-click to collapse or expand, resize from the lower-right, or close it with ×, Close, or R.

Introduction: This web app is meant to duplicate the ease and speed of drawing in a room on a whiteboard. It mostly sticks to the essentials and has numerous shortcuts built in for speed. The workflow is optimized for quickly switching between line drawing and text entry.

Keyboard shortcuts:

  • ` toggles Draw and Text and returns Select to Draw; W toggles Wash; T toggles the Light/Dark theme; S activates the currently chosen Rectangle or Free-form selection mode, and a quick second press switches between them while keeping Select active; L strokes an active marquee with the current color and line width; E opens movable Selection effects for a selection or pending image: Blur, current-color Shadow, Tonal controls (brightness and contrast), and Color controls (grayscale, sepia, invert, saturation, and hue rotation); Escape always returns to Draw.
  • Shift constrains drawing to horizontal, vertical, and 45-degree lines, and snaps image rotation; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift draws a square box; Ctrl/Cmd+Alt draws a rectangle from its starting corner; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+X draws a perfect circle; and Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+X draws an ellipse. F flips the active image left-to-right, then bottom-to-top, right-to-left, and top-to-bottom; the four-step sequence repeats. M toggles Magic, rotating its gradient every .75 seconds. Arrow keys—or 1/2/3/4 for left/right/up/down outside text entry—change color or size.
  • Ctrl/Cmd+C / Ctrl/Cmd+X / Ctrl/Cmd+V copy, cut, and paste a selection; Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y or Cmd+Z / Shift+Cmd+Z undo and redo.
  • Delete erases the active selection or exits pending art or text entry; C opens Clear confirmation; R restores default tools and settings, including Rectangle Select, and closes floating Help.
  • Pending art: While positioning imported or pasted art, or a selection moved away from its source, the Arrow keys nudge it by 1px instead of changing color or size. Press Enter to commit imported, pasted, or moved selection art after resizing.

Drawing, Fill, and color: Choose Draw, Text, or Fill. Select a preset color or use the color picker, then click for a dot or drag to draw. The eyedropper button beside the Zoom field previews the color and hex value under its cursor; click to sample that color, which also turns Wash off. Press Escape to cancel eyedropper mode. Wash uses 30% transparent color; swatches preview it with their full-color outer border. The circular cursor previews the mark size. Activate Fill to turn Draw off, then click a contained area to flood it with the current color. Click Fill again to return to Draw. Its plus-and-bucket cursor uses the selected color; Wash affects its colored areas while the black outline stays solid.

Lines and shapes: Hold Shift while drawing to snap a line to horizontal, vertical, or 45-degree directions. Hold the shape keys before starting a drag: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift makes a square, Ctrl/Cmd+Alt makes a rectangle, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+X makes a perfect circle, and Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+X makes an ellipse. Shapes start from the point where you begin the drag.

Text: Choose Text, then click the canvas and type. Drag the Move icon at the left of the text field to reposition it; release the pointer to drop it. Press Enter to place the text, or click elsewhere on the canvas to place it and start another text box. The I-beam previews its size.

Images, selection and files: The Import Picture button imports images as a movable preview, fitting large images within the canvas without enlarging smaller ones. With an active selection, an imported image retains its original pixel size and is cropped to the selection; a free-form selection uses its lasso shape. Clicking away or pressing Enter restores that selection around the placed image so it can be moved. Drag the image to position it, use corner handles to resize proportionally, edge handles to stretch it, or the round handle above it to rotate. Hold Shift while rotating to snap in 90-degree increments from the starting position. Double-click restores its aspect ratio; click outside or press Enter to place it. Use the arrow beside Select to choose Rectangle or Free-form, or double-click the Select icon to switch styles, then press S or click the Select icon. Free-form traces an open lasso while dragging and closes on release; its handles appear only after it is moved. Resize an unmoved selection to change its marquee without stretching pixels, or drag it and use the handles to scale its contents; press Enter to commit a moved or resized selection. The rotate handle appears after moving or flipping a selection. Flipping a lasso converts it to a rectangular selection. A pasted selection opens as an import-style preview. Clear asks for confirmation, and Download PNG saves a timestamped PNG; in Dark theme, choose the dark canvas background or white. Mouse, touch, and pen input are supported.

Toolbar actions: Use the Import Image icon to add art and the Download PNG icon to save your work. When zoomed in, the navigator shows a rough canvas preview; drag its viewport to pan, or close it to return to 100%. Clear sits at the far right of the toolbar and asks for confirmation before removing the canvas.

Preferences: Open Preferences beside Instructions to choose the beginning color source, default selection mode, default Wash setting, whether More controls opens, the default Light or Dark theme, initial line width, and initial font size. Saving stores these settings in this browser; Restore defaults fills the dialog with the app defaults without saving them.

Floating Help: Choose Float at the bottom of this dialog to keep Help visible above the canvas while editing. Drag its title bar to reposition it, double-click the title bar to collapse or expand the panel, and resize it from the lower-right corner. Close it with the × in its title bar, the Close button in its lower-right corner, or R.

Cookie information: Sketch Pad stores your saved preferences in a cookie named sketchPadPreferences. It contains only your tool settings (color, selection mode, Wash, More controls, theme, line width, and text size), is available only to this site, and expires after one year.
