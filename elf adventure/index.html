Elf behavior

Bring gifts to the elves' cottages to earn their trust and jewels. The game pauses while this window is open.

Emotions and trust

Elves start Neutral. Sentiment ranges from Sad (1) to Happy (3); Attitude ranges from Trust (1) to Disgust (3). Use Show emotions to see their current feelings.

A liked gift raises Sentiment by one; a hated gift lowers it by one. Neutral items do not change Sentiment. After assessing two items, an elf earns Trust, even if those items are hated or neutral. Elves return home to assess new or moved items anywhere inside their cottage.

Elves keep one of each liked item in their treasures. They dump hated items in the nearest room outside their home area: normally Willow Crossing for the red and blue elves, or Moonwell Vault for the green and purple elves. Extra liked items go to The Crossroads. Neutral items stay where you leave them.

Roaming and movement

Elves roam between rooms but do not forage for loose gifts. Bring items inside their cottages for assessment. They step away from doorways and walk around obstacles. If you block an elf's route to a gift, it pauses briefly before trying again; moving away lets it continue immediately.

Jewels and possessions

An elf with Trust offers its jewel in the gift area when it has assessed two items or is Happy. It pauses for one second before collecting the jewel to share it. If Trust is lost, it tries to return the jewel to its protected corner while the jewel remains inside its cottage. Taking one of its possessions lowers Sentiment by one.

A jewel in the protected corner is locked until its owner is knocked out. After the owner recovers, the protection returns.

Axes and chase personalities

Each axe can be thrown only once, by you or an elf. After hitting something or finishing its flight, it stays visible for 0.4 seconds, then plays the Gaster disappearance sound and fades out over 0.6 seconds. A used axe cannot be picked up again. Putting down an axe without throwing it keeps it usable.

An axe hit makes an elf Sad and Disgusted: it looks surprised for 0.4 seconds, then stays knocked out for 30 seconds. After recovering, a Sad and Disgusted elf hunts you and finds an axe. Elves move 10% faster than their original speed. Disgusted elves move another 20% faster than that new base speed. Sad alone does not change their speed.

A hunting elf looks for its own unused axe first, then another reachable unused axe. It carries the axe on its left or right side and throws from that position. Once it uses that axe, it must find another.

  • Rhaelin (Red) — Bold: closes in.
  • Galenor (Green) — Cautious: keeps more distance and approaches at an angle.
  • Belthir (Blue) — Flanker: approaches from the side.
  • Pelendil (Purple) — Stalker: hangs back and approaches at an angle.

Chasing elves spread apart and choose separate throwing positions. They throw within 400 pixels when they have a clear shot, moving around obstacles to find an angle. An elf's axe hitting you ends the game. A new or moved item inside its cottage interrupts hunting so it can assess the item. Hunting stops when the elf is no longer both Sad and Disgusted.
