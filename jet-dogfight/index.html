Arcade Dogfight

Skyfire Jets

Fly a fighter jet across a moonlit sky, dodge incoming fire, and bring down every enemy plane before they can shoot you out of the air.

Hero Hull 3
Missiles 1
Enemies Left 1
Shots Fired 0

Controls: Up or W to speed up, Down to slow down, Left or A to turn left, Right or D to turn right, Space to fire, E to launch a missile, F to drop rear flares, P to pause, R to restart at any time. Shoot down every enemy plane before they hit you or ram you.

Press any flight key to launch level 1.
