SKY STRIKE -

Level 1 · Score: 0 · Hull: 99 · Boss: 150

Score points by bombing green tanks for 10, red tanks for 20, barns for 10, blimps for 20, and enemy raiders for 30. At 150 points, the Boss Barn arrives after a short warning; destroy it to clear Level 1. Press New mission or N to play Level 2 at night. Blue repair pickups restore hull; avoid enemy fire and collisions.
